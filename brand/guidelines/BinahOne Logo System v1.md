# Purpose

This document defines the official logo architecture for BinahOne and establishes the principles, structure, variants, deliverables and governance rules that will guide the creation, usage and evolution of the visual identity.

The objective of the Logo System is to ensure consistency across digital, commercial, marketing and institutional touchpoints.

This document must be approved before the creation of the final production-ready vector assets.

---

# Logo Design Principles

The BinahOne logo must communicate:

- Clarity
- Confidence
- Precision
- Scalability
- Structure
- Modern Enterprise
- Intelligent Business Technology

The logo should feel:

- Professional
- Contemporary
- Trustworthy
- Technological
- Human-centered

The logo should not feel:

- Corporate legacy
- Startup hype
- Cyberpunk
- Crypto-centric
- Gaming-oriented
- Artificially futuristic

---

# Core Symbol

## Name

**b1 Monogram**

---

## Concept

The symbol combines:

- **b** → Binah
- **1** → One

Conceptually representing:

**Insight → Execution → Growth**

This directly reflects the BinahOne positioning and brand promise.

---

## Symbol Characteristics

The symbol should be:

- Geometric
- Minimalist
- Memorable
- Balanced
- Scalable

Visual construction should follow:

- Consistent grid
- Consistent stroke weight
- Unified corner radius
- Optical balance
- Clear legibility

---

## Design Direction

### Approved Direction

**Enterprise Minimalism**

Inspired by the clarity and simplicity found in modern digital brands such as:

- Stripe
- Linear
- Mercury
- Ramp
- Notion

The objective is not to imitate these brands, but to achieve a similar level of clarity, professionalism and visual maturity.

# Logo Architecture

The BinahOne Logo System consists of four official constructions.

---

## Primary Logo

**Structure**

```text
[b1] BinahOne
```

**Purpose**

Primary institutional signature.

**Applications**

- Corporate website
- Business proposals
- Brand presentations
- Official documents
- Marketing materials

---

## Horizontal Logo

**Structure**

```text
[b1] BinahOne
```

Optimized for horizontal spaces.

**Applications**

- Header navigation
- Website menus
- Software navigation bars
- Email signature

---

## Stacked Logo

**Structure**

```text
[b1]
BinahOne
```

Optimized for vertical layouts.

**Applications**

- Presentation covers
- Social graphics
- Reports
- Printed materials

---

## Symbol Only

**Structure**

```text
[b1]
```

Used when space is limited or where the symbol alone is sufficient.

**Applications**

- Favicon
- Application icon
- Social profile image
- Watermark
- Product avatar

---

# Wordmark

## Official Name

The official company name is:

**BinahOne**

---

## Incorrect Variations

The following variations must not be used as institutional replacements:

- BINAH ONE
- Binah One
- binahone
- B1

## Wordmark Characteristics

The final wordmark should be custom refined and must not appear to be simply typed using a standard font.

The official wordmark should include:

- Optical spacing adjustments
- Custom proportions
- Professional refinement
- Consistent alignment with the symbol

A clear space must always exist around the logo.

No text, graphics or interface elements may enter the protected area.

The exact measurement will be defined during vector construction and documented in the final logo specification.

# Incorrect Usage

The following actions are prohibited.

## Do Not Stretch

Never alter proportions.

---

## Do Not Recolor

Never apply unauthorized colors.

---

## Do Not Add Effects

Never add:

- Shadows
- Glows
- Bevels
- Outlines
- 3D effects

---

## Do Not Modify Structure

Never:

- Redraw elements
- Change spacing
- Reposition symbol and wordmark

---

## Do Not Use PNG as Source

PNG files are export formats.

Official master assets must remain vector-based.

---

# Governance

The logo system follows the official hierarchy below.

## Source of Truth

1. Figma
2. brand/logos/master
3. brand/exports
4. public/logos

---

## Rule

Files inside:

```text
public/logos
```

must never be edited directly.

Published assets must always be generated from approved master files.
