# Symbol Construction

## Symbol Name

b1 Monogram

---

## Construction Philosophy

The symbol must be drawn as a monogram.

The symbol is not the juxtaposition of two independent characters.

Incorrect:

b + 1

Correct:

b1

as a single visual unit.

---

# Grid System

Base Grid:

8 × 8

Refinement Grid:

16 × 16

All geometry must align to the construction grid.

---

# Container

Shape:

Rounded Square

Ratio:

1:1

Corner Radius:

20% of container width

Example:

100 × 100

Corner Radius:

20

---

# Monogram Construction

## Stroke Weight

Target Range:

10% to 14% of container width

Example:

Container = 100

Stroke = 12

---

## Optical Center

The monogram shall be visually centered.

Optical balance is more important than mathematical center alignment.

---

## b Geometry

Characteristics:

- Geometric
- Rounded
- Open counterform
- High legibility

The bowl must remain recognizable at 16px.

---

## 1 Geometry

Characteristics:

- Minimal
- Vertical
- Derived from the same stroke language

The numeral must visually belong to the same construction system as the b.
